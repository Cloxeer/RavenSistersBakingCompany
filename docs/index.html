How our drops work

We bake in limited batches from a home kitchen, so everything is made to order and always fresh. Drops open on Hotplate and usually sell out.

  1. 1

    The menu goes up

    We post the week's line-up on Hotplate and Instagram, and the order window opens.

  2. 2

    Claim your order

    Order and pay through Hotplate — checkout is handled securely by Square.

  3. 3

    Pickup or delivery

    We confirm the pickup window and delivery area for that drop, then bake it fresh that morning.
